## Brand & Style

The brand represents an elevated luxury hair atelier specializing in bespoke wig artistry, couture protective styling, and holistic textured hair care. It speaks to an affluent, discerning clientele that values structural perfection, deliberate artistry, and timeless luxury. The emotional response is one of private salon exclusivity, quiet prestige, and editorial poise—evoking the rarefied atmosphere of an intimate Parisian dressing suite combined with modern high-fashion beauty.

The design style merges **High-Fashion Editorial Minimalism** with delicate **Tactile Luxury Accents**. The interface mirrors a physical beauty folio: generous warm cream whitespace, dramatic typographic contrast, razor-thin gold hairlines, and deliberate restraint over ornamental clutter. Visual density is kept low; photography of sculptural braids, luminous silk presses, and immaculate lace melts commands the screen like archival magazine spreads.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in warm heritage tones, balancing deep espresso density against luminous pale linens and brushed metallic accents.

- **Primary (`#C6A36A` - Champagne Gold):** Used for focal interactive actions, active states, key metadata badges, and high-priority appointments.
- **Secondary (`#171312` - Espresso Black):** The primary ink for editorial titles, deep button states, contrast banners, and luxury text hierarchy. Never use pitch-black `#000000`; espresso black maintains a velvet-like warmth.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Warm Gold):** Applied with disciplined restraint for delicate hover transitions, metallic borders, rating stars, and subtle iconography.
- **Neutral (`#F8F3EC` - Warm Cream):** The foundation canvas. It eliminates stark clinical whites, offering an organic silk-paper ground that flatters high-resolution textured hair and skin photography.
- **Supporting Surfaces & Borders:** Soft beige (`#E8DCCB`) functions as the container layer for service detail cards and booking step wells, while muted taupe (`#DFD5C6`) provides gossamer 1px borders that evoke bookbinding precision.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic publication grandeur and pristine technical legibility.

- **Editorial Headings:** Set in *Playfair Display*, using delicate regular and medium weights. Large display titles utilize tightened tracking to highlight the high-contrast serifs, emulating mastheads from prestige fashion periodicals.
- **Editorial Sub-details & Body:** Handled by *Plus Jakarta Sans*. Body text enjoys open line heights to maximize readability across warm parchment backgrounds, maintaining an airy, effortless rhythm.
- **Micro-Labels & Categorization:** Overline descriptors, treatment categories, and price indicators utilize `label-caps`—all-caps, small-size, wide-tracked styling to establish clear hierarchy without screaming for attention.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of 1440px to sustain an intentional editorial composition on ultra-wide screens.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views maintain a generous 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 64px outer section margins, creating framing borders reminiscent of art books. Tablet displays reduce to an 8-column layout (40px margins), while mobile breaks into a 4-column layout (20px margins, 16px gutters).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Spacing values scale geometrically. Editorial hero units and service showcase sections use exaggerated vertical margins (up to 6rem–8rem between blocks) to allow high-contrast styling portraits room to breathe.
- **Reflow Rules:** Asymmetrical split-screens (editorial image left, bespoke booking details right) stack cleanly on screens narrower than 1024px, placing imagery above typography to retain visual allure.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with high-fashion print aesthetics, visual hierarchy is communicated primarily through **tonal paper layering** and **delicate hairlines** rather than synthetic floating drop shadows.

- **Layering System:** Base canvas begins at `#F8F3EC` (Warm Cream). Elevated panels (e.g., service detail drawers, bespoke quote modules, elevated atelier cards) rely on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft beige (`#E8DCCB`) surfaces.
- **Ghost Linework:** Structural depth is articulated by ultra-crisp 1px borders using `#DFD5C6` or semi-transparent champagne lines (`rgba(198, 163, 106, 0.35)`).
- **Micro-Ambient Cast:** Floating interactive components (such as sticky booking navigation pills or bespoke client consultation modals) utilize an ultra-diffused, barely-there ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -10px rgba(23, 19, 18, 0.06)`. Shadows are warm-tinted via the espresso base color to avoid cold gray undertones.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** shape hierarchy. Architectural components, service panels, and imagery utilize structured, near-square corners (4px radius) that mirror crisp magazine framing and perfume packaging. 

To introduce human balance against these structured grids, interactive tokens such as service category tags, curation chips, and the primary atelier booking button embrace specialized **capsule-pill contours** (`9999px`). This intentional duality ensures the structure remains authoritative and sharp, while interaction touchpoints appear refined and approachable.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve Atelier Appointment"):** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), filled with espresso black (`#171312`) with champagne gold text (`#C6A36A`) or rich champagne gold background (`#C6A36A`) with espresso typography. Hover triggers a subtle expansion and micro-shift toward warm gold (`#D4AF37`) with a 1px border glow.
- **Secondary CTA ("Explore Collection"):** Pill-shaped transparent button bordered by a 1px solid stroke in `#C6A36A`, paired with body-weight uppercase type and generous horizontal padding (`2rem`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Used for hair textures (e.g., *4C Silk Press*, *Virgin Raw Cambodian*, *Boho Goddess Braids*). Rendered with a full pill curve, 1px `#DFD5C6` border, `#FFFFFF` interior, and `label-caps` typography. The selected state swaps to `#171312` fill with `#F8F3EC` text.

### Atelier Service Cards
- Surfaces rendered in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#DFD5C6` outline and 4px soft corners. Photography spans full-width with a classic 4:5 editorial portrait ratio. Pricing, estimated duration, and stylist tiers sit below with crisp hairline dividers separating metadata rows.

### Form Inputs & Booking Fields
- Clean underline-only or soft-boxed styles. Background is `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#DFD5C6` perimeter. Focus states shift the stroke to `#C6A36A` without aggressive drop shadows. Placeholder typography is set in `#171312` at 40% opacity in `body-md`.

### Hair Consultation Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom-crafted minimal circles and squares. Radios adopt an espresso outer ring with an inner concentric champagne circle when active. Checkboxes use razor-thin gold checks on white backdrops.

### Additional Signature Component: Editorial Lookbook Splitter
- A dual-panel showcase component featuring an auto-advancing full-bleed image on the left and an editorial critique on the right with typographic hair texture index, customized braid lengths, and a direct "Book This Style" quick-action pill.
